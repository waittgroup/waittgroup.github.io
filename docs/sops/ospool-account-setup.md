# OSPool Account Setup

This is the SOP for getting an OSPool account, which is required
before running any computational workflow on OSPool (ORCA, BLAST, ML
training, etc.). Don't start this on your own — it begins with the PI.

## 1. Wait to be directed by the PI

The PI will tell you when it's time to request an OSPool account.
Don't register before then — account requests are tied to a group
orientation session (see step 3).

## 2. Register for an OSPool account

Once directed, go to the official OSPool registration guide and follow
its steps:
[portal.osg-htc.org/documentation/overview/account_setup/registration-and-login](https://portal.osg-htc.org/documentation/overview/account_setup/registration-and-login/)

## 3. Attend the facilitation team orientation

The PI will arrange a group meeting with the OSPool facilitation
(orientation) team. Everyone requesting an account attends this
meeting together.

## 4. Finish account setup after approval

After the orientation meeting, once the facilitation team approves the
group's accounts, follow the remaining account-creation procedures on
the OSPool website (same link as step 2) to finish setting up your
account.

## 5. Meet with your PI to learn your workflow

Once your account is active, meet with the PI to learn how to use
OSPool for your specific research project. Which SOP you need depends
on your project:

- [ORCA on OSPool](orca-ospool-workflow.md) — quantum chemistry
  calculations
- BLAST on OSPool — sequence search _(SOP coming soon)_
- ML on OSPool — model training _(SOP coming soon)_
